1. Summary of Mandated Field Audit Compliance Standards

Compliance Standard Mandated In-Store Operational Procedure Enforcement & Verification Mechanism
1. Live In-Store Verification Every audit must be conducted physically inside the retail outlet. Supervisors must log GPS coordinates, timestamp, and live store photo. Strictly camera-only capture; photo gallery uploads are blocked by system policy. GPS radius validation.
2. Power SKU On-Shelf Availability Supervisors must inspect and record stock availability for canonical revenue drivers: 24 Modern Trade SKUs and 27 Traditional Trade SKUs. Dynamic availability scoring on executive dashboards; immediate out-of-stock alert dispatch to van sales reps.
3. Asset Purity & Cold Chain Verify 100% brand purity in company chillers and freezers. Zero tolerance for competitor items or warm operating temperatures. Mandatory temperature probe recording (-18°C for Ice Cream, 0°C–8°C for Dairy); flagged breach alerts to Fleet team.
4. Asset Branding & Damage Reporting Inspect condition of cooler decals, canopy lightbox, and exterior paint. Log branding requests with mandatory condition photos. Dedicated Admin review portal; automatic PowerPoint (.pptx) and Excel (.xlsx) work orders generated for branding vendors.
5. GM Corrective Directives When leadership flags a merchandising flaw, assigned supervisor must acknowledge the directive and submit camera photo proof of resolution. Mandatory 24–48 hour resolution SLA; issues cannot be closed without verified photographic proof.
6. Tiered Visit Frequency SLAs Execute route schedules per classification SOP: Class A: 4 visits/month (bi-weekly minimum), Class B: 2 visits/month, Class C: 1 visit/month, Class D: per PJP. Automated frequency tracker banner on Admin Dashboard; alerts management to under-visited high-volume accounts.
