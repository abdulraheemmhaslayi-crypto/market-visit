3. The Revenue Formula: How the Platform Directly Increases FMCG Sales

1. Eradication of Out-of-Stock (OOS) on High-Velocity Power SKUs Estimated Revenue Lift: +5% to +8%

In FMCG fresh dairy and beverages, impulse consumption is massive. When a consumer cannot find Dandy Laban, Fresh Milk, or Ice Cream, they immediately buy a competitor's product. By forcing supervisors to verify canonical Power SKUs (24 MT / 27 TT) on every visit, stock depletion is detected and replenished before peak shopping hours, directly recovering otherwise lost transactions.

2. Defending Cooler Purity & Maximizing Refrigerated Share-of-Space Estimated Revenue Lift: +3% to +5%

Cold drinks and ice creams sell only when cold. The app's multi-chiller/freezer audit checks operating temperatures and detects unauthorized non-Dandy items occupying company equipment. Regaining 20% to 30% of encroached cooler space directly translates into more facings for high-margin Dandy items, accelerating impulse purchase velocity.

3. Enforcing 80/20 Account Frequency Discipline (Classification SLAs) Estimated Revenue Lift: +4% to +6%

In FMCG distribution, the Pareto Principle dominates: the top 20% of accounts (Class A Hypermarkets and Supermarkets) drive 80% of sales volume. The platform's real-time frequency tracker ensures that Class A accounts are visited at least 4 times per month without exception. Preventing service gaps in top-tier accounts protects primary sales volume from competitive erosion.

4. Closed-Loop Problem Resolution via GM Directives Estimated Operational Efficiency: +25% Faster Turnaround

In traditional setups, field merchandising defects (damaged POSM, expired stock, displaced shelves) take weeks to resolve through email chains. The platform connects GM directives directly to supervisors with mandatory photo proof of resolution. Issues that previously bled revenue for 20 days are now corrected within 24 to 48 hours.

5. Enterprise Governance, Fraud Prevention & Field Accountability

Integrity Controls Built into the Platform

  • Camera-Only Enforcement: Supervisors cannot upload pre-saved images from the phone gallery; only real-time camera captures are accepted, eliminating fabricated audit photos.
  • Geotagged & Timestamped Submissions: Every visit capture and directive closure record includes validated location and time markers.
  • Documented Bypass Logs: If a scheduled store cannot be audited, supervisors must log official reasons (Shop Closed, Refused) with mandatory photo proof, preventing route negligence.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions separating Admin, Sub-Admin, and Supervisors, preserving commercial master data integrity.
  • Dual-Engine Enterprise Cloud Architecture: Hosted on Microsoft Azure App Service with Azure SQL Server and automated CI/CD pipeline ensuring enterprise uptime and reliability.
